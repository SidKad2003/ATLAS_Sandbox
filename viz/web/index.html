How to read the map
Coloured circles are agents. Shoppers have their own colours and start at the Homes; the Shopping Assistant (A) sits at the Help desk, Fraud Review (F) in the Fraud office, SRE / Ops (S) in the Ops room, and human approvers (H) at the Approval desk.
Labels above an agent say what they just did: 🔍 SEARCH, 👀 VIEW, 🛒 + BASKET, 📦 DELIVERY?, 💳 CHECKOUT, ✅ ORDER ✓, ❌ FAILED, 🛡 BLOCKED.
Travelling tokens show work passing between agents: 🤝 case (assistant to fraud), 🧾 approval (agent to a human), 📦 order (checkout to warehouse). 💬 bubbles and dashed lines are chats.
Red pulsing places have an injected fault. Badges show 🧾 approvals waiting for a human, 🚨 incidents, ✅ orders and 🤝 fraud cases. 🛒 next to a shopper means items in the basket; 💤 means resting at home.
Click any agent or any line in the event log to highlight it; in Replay, clicking a line jumps the map to that moment.
